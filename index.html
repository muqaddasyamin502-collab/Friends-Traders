About Friends Trader

Your Trusted Kitchenware Partner Since 2021

Friends Trader is a premier kitchen utensils and cookware store located in the heart of Multan. With over 25 years of combined experience in the kitchenware industry, we provide high-quality products that combine durability, functionality, and style.

We offer a wide range of kitchen utensils, cookware sets, bakeware, cutlery, and specialty items for both home cooks and professional chefs. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted name in Multan.
